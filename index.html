(Not a) Password Manager

Deterministically generate a password.


Passphrase

A memerable, easy passphrase that is unique for every password you generate. For example: To get your Amazon.com password your passphrase here could be "jungle".

Salt and Reset Counter

Salt ensures that this password is unique to you - even if someone else uses the same passphrase. This can - and probably should - be the same for every password you generate.

The Reset counter is how many times you've changed your password. Incrementing this will give you a completely new password, but you don't have to memorize a new passphrase or salt value.
