[NOT A] PASSWORD MANAGER
This is a deterministic password generator. This means that none of your information is stored on a server somewhere. Instead, passwords are generated (by your browser) according to set rules based on your login phrase and the name of the platform you are logging in to. If you want the technical details of how this works, check out the GitHub page.
No worries! You don't have to "create" an account - just login with a word or phrase that you will easily remember later. (But not something that others would easily guess - don't use your name, for example.) Your account credentials are never stored. See ABOUT for more information.
